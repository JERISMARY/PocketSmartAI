Cookie Policy

Last updated: October 2024

1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your device when you visit our website. They help us make the site work properly and provide a better user experience.

2. How We Use Cookies

We primarily use cookies for Authentication. When you log in to PocketSmart AI, we set a secure, HTTP-only cookie (`access_token`) that keeps you logged in as you navigate between the different planners (Home, Party, Jewelry) and your dashboard.

3. Essential Cookies Only

Currently, we only use strictly necessary cookies required for the application to function (authentication and session management). We do not use third-party tracking or advertising cookies.

4. Managing Cookies

You can instruct your browser to refuse all cookies or to indicate when a cookie is being sent. However, if you do not accept cookies, you will not be able to log in or use the AI planning features of our service.
