Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We've got answers.

How does the AI budget planner work?

We use Google's Gemini AI to analyze your inputs (like budget, style, and images) to generate a realistic budget breakdown and curated product recommendations tailored to your specific needs.

Is it free to use?

Yes! We offer a generous Free tier that allows you to generate up to 5 AI budget plans per day. If you need more, you can upgrade to our Pro plan.

Are the estimated prices accurate?

The prices provided are estimates based on current market trends for the Indian market (₹). Actual prices may vary depending on the specific brand or retailer you choose.

Is my data secure?

Yes, your data is securely stored. We use industry-standard encryption, and your uploaded images are only processed for the duration of the AI analysis and are not stored permanently.
